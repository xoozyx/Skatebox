MINI RAMP

MENUS

Press any button (or tap the game) to start. ESC or the MENU button opens the menu: pick the level and your outfit (UP / DOWN choose a row, LEFT / RIGHT change it, SPACE or ESC to go back). A run in progress is paused, and changing the level starts a new run. F1: this help (the game pauses).

RIDING

LEFT / RIGHT pump on the ramp. UP / DOWN ride toward the back / front of the ramp (while rolling fast or in the air).

AIR TRICKS

A kickflip. A+RIGHT heelflip, A+DOWN shove-it, A+UP FS shove-it. Hold A or tap again for double, triple, quad flips and 360, 540... shove-its.

D grab. D+DOWN indy, D+UP melon, D+RIGHT nose, D+LEFT tail.

S spin. Hold S to keep spinning (every 180 counts, up to 1440), release to finish the turn. Each tap also adds 180.

Every 180 spin turns him around: his back is shown until the next 180 (he rides switch). A wipeout resets his stance.

Landing in the middle of a trick is a wipeout.

COPING (press W near the top of the wall)

Moving toward the back or front of the ramp (UP / DOWN held): W 50-50 grind, W+RIGHT nose slide, W+LEFT tail slide. They slide along the coping.

Not moving that way (for example hovering above the coping): W rock stall, W+RIGHT nose stall, W+LEFT tail stall. Keep the balance marker off the far end with LEFT / RIGHT.

Release W to drop back in. You need enough speed to reach the coping, and each wall allows one stall and one slide until you ride across the flat bottom.

OPTIONS

Your level, outfit and touch-button setting are saved in this browser.
